Our Promise

100% Handmade, From Start to Finish

No shortcuts. Just traditional techniques, skilled hands and authentic artistry. Every single Art Bloom garment — saree, kurta, shirt or tee — passes through the hands of a traditional artisan at every stage.

Hand-Painted

Every motif is first painted by hand onto paper, referencing generations-old folk-art patterns.

Hand-Carved Blocks

Artisans carve teak printing blocks by hand, one motif at a time, no two blocks ever identical.

Hand Block-Printed

Each block is dipped in natural dye and hand-stamped onto fabric, stroke by careful stroke.

Hand-Stitched

Local tailors cut and stitch every garment to size — the final human touch before it reaches you.

Our Story

Where heritage
finds a new silhouette

Art Bloom began with a simple question: why should centuries-old folk art live only on walls and canvases? We travel to villages across Gujarat, Bihar, Andhra Pradesh and Rajasthan, working directly with artisan families who still carve their own printing blocks and mix their own natural dyes.

Every saree, kurta, shirt and tee we make is 100% hand-made and hand-crafted — not a digital reproduction, but the real imperfect, expressive touch of art made by hand.
